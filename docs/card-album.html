The Card Album

Cards are not bought here. They are found — a day witnessed on the Moons page, a word heard on its own study page, a Spark met — and kept on this device, in this album. Every card has two faces: the collection face, and the game face it will play with when the resonance game opens. Nothing on this shelf gates anything real; a card is a keepsake beside the act, never the price of it.

The First Card

Struck at the meeting itself. It opens the album, and it never moves.

The Thirteen Moons

One card for each Moon, found by witnessing a day in that Moon. A Moon's window is its own season.

The Words

The eight studied Hebrew words. Hear a word sounded on its own study page, and its card answers.

The Spark Kinds

The six kinds. Your own kind's card is earned at the meeting; the rest are met on roads still being built.
