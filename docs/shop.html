Forge Process

How an Artifact Enters the Registry

The registry records origin, material, handwork, naming, witness, and release.

1 Reclaim Material is found, gathered, gifted, salvaged, or repurposed.
2 Shape Copper, stone, sea glass, all are formed by hand.
3 Name One-of-one pieces receive a title, code, collection, and role. With possible future updates to each piece.
4 Record Photos, notes, materials, finish, and story enter the registry.
5 Release The artifact becomes available, gifted, archived, or kept personal. Every piece is made intuitively by design.
Handmade variation: Hammer marks, hand shaping, natural patina, and individual asymmetry are part of the artifact’s identity. Repeatable series such as TB remain recognizable, but no two hand-forged pieces are exact machine copies. While supplies last copper comes from the QRI-16 V.A.R.C.E Reality Engine. Before You Order

Shipping, Timing & Returns

Plain words, the way this shop actually works — read them before you write, and keep your confirmation email: it is your order's whole record.

Timing Every piece is made to order or finished by hand after your order is confirmed. Your timing is given in writing in that confirmation, before you pay — if the forge cannot meet it, you hear so before any money moves, never after.
Photographed before it ships Before a piece leaves, it is photographed and entered in the registry under its code. The registry record — origin, material, handwork, release — is your piece's provenance, and it stays standing on this page.
Shipping Pieces ship as tracked parcels to addresses in the United States, and the shipping cost is named in your written confirmation — never a surprise added at the door. Outside the United States: ask first by email, and it is confirmed case by case before any payment.
If something is wrong If a piece arrives damaged, or does not match what was confirmed — sizing, finish, orientation — write promptly on delivery, with a photo if it helps. Aaron will repair it, reforge it, or refund it: the maker's word is the warranty, and it is kept.
Returns Made-to-order work is made for one person's wrist and measure, so change-of-mind returns are not taken on custom pieces. The confirmation step exists for exactly this reason: the piece is right on paper before it exists in copper. Nothing here limits any right your own law gives you.
Copper, honestly Natural copper darkens and takes a patina as it is worn — that is the metal being itself, and the sealed finish slows it. Copper can leave a green mark on skin for some wearers; it washes off and harms nothing. If that would bother you, choose the sealed finish at confirmation.
