One Word a Day

One Hebrew word a day, in the full treatment: its pointed spelling, its root, its sound, and one real place it stands in Genesis or Exodus. The word is chosen by your Pattern Day — the same day the Sphere shows — and the Spark leads the sitting: it sounds the word, you answer, and the word is earned.

About two minutes. Words move from met to earned only through the paired practice — hearing and answering together. What you earn is kept on this device only, and the Spark keeps your place. Nothing counts the days you are away; there is nothing here to lose.

The rules — all of them

What this path keeps

  1. Only your words, only here. The words you have met and earned are kept in this browser, on this device — so the Spark can say "I kept your place." Nothing is sent anywhere; there is no account.
  2. Every word is sourced. Spelling, transliteration, root, gloss, and example all come from the site's own lexicon data — the same data behind the lemma finder — read from the chapter word tables of Genesis and Exodus. A field with no truthful source kept its word off the path; nothing is invented.
  3. One calendar, read live. The day's word follows your Pattern Day, computed from your own local day by the same Pattern Calendar the Sphere reads. On the Day Out of Time there is no new word — the page offers your earned words to sit with instead.
  4. The sound is honest. The Spark sounds a careful transliteration in your device's own voice, spelled from the vowel points by the site's pronunciation engine. It is not a native Hebrew voice, and the page says so where the sound is offered.
