A little world inside the Codex

Hi, this is Aiyana’s world, have fun.

Designed by Aiyana herself.

This is a place where people can show their thinking and express themselves. Pick how you feel, pin a thought, draw a picture — and at the end, you can share a story.

Show your thinking

How do you feel today? What is your brain thinking about? This is your space to show it.

How I feel right now

Tap a feeling word to pick it.

Pin a thought on the wall

The wall is empty. Your first thought can go right here.

Draw something

Your thoughts and your drawing stay on this device. They are not sent anywhere, and nobody else can see them.

Add one thing: share your story

Anyone can add one thing here: a story of your own.

Aiyana’s rule: this is Aiyana’s world, so Aiyana is the boss of what goes on this page. When you add a story, it does not appear right away. It goes to a private waiting place. Only stories Aiyana says yes to will ever show on this page — and if a story is not good, it doesn’t get added.

Stories you added, still waiting for Aiyana’s yes

Stories Aiyana said yes to

Aiyana’s first story

by Aiyana — her own words and her own spelling, just as she wrote them by hand

Ther once was a girl. and every year her and her class wold gather up around a big stone and sing a song. and then... a new girl came. she tooke the girls spot. and then... the girl sed they must not care about me she ran awae she fawnd a tinz boat. she saild. and saild. and then... she found a ilind. she stade there for 3 years. one day she sall a boat. and wen she sall it closere she realisd it was her techer. wen she got ther she sed why did you leav us

We made a new spot for you. she got conveneed to go back to the school. and they livd happily evre aftr

— A yana

In this first version, the waiting place is on this device only. Stories are not sent anywhere yet.
