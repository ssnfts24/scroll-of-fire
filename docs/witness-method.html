The Witness Method

The day is being read…

Every claim asks to be believed. This short lesson teaches the five labels the Codex sorts claims by — and then lets you practise on real claims from the Old World investigation, including the ones that failed their check. The Spark walks with you, one claim at a time. Your place is kept on this device only; nothing is scored, and nothing follows you.

The Spark is reading the day…

Step one — the five labels

Before any claim is weighed, it is named. Each label says what kind of thing the claim is, and how much weight it can honestly carry. Every example below wears its own label — the lesson obeys its own method.

Step two — the practice

Eight claims, all drawn from the Old World investigation. Read each one and place it under its label. When you place it, the lesson shows the investigation's own reasoning. A wrong shelf is not a failure — the Spark offers a hint, and you look again. Take as many tries as the claim needs.

You placed them all. Notice what changed: not the claims — your eye. That is the whole method.

    Step three — the ladder

    Under the labels stands the ladder every piece of work in the Codex climbs — or refuses to climb.

      The rule: nothing gets quietly upgraded. A claim may climb only when new evidence is named — the way the earthquake's magnitude stays tied to the publication that states it, instead of hardening into a number nobody can check.

      Where the checking lands

      After all of it, here is what the Old World investigation itself can say — and how carefully it says it:

      Verified Record Across 1850–1930, fires, fairs, asylums, and the moving of children overlap in the documented record. That overlap is a documented correlation.

      Verified Record Coordination between them is not documented in the sources opened.

      A pattern is not causation. A correlation is documented — and that is all it is, until new evidence is named. The first sentence is what the record holds; the second is the record's honest edge. Nothing was quietly upgraded to get there.
