The whole site, drawn as one road

The Traveller's Map

Every place on this site is a land on one road — the road of The Traveller's Road, the story told a chapter at a time across the whole Codex. The lands below are pictured by their own cartoon scenes. A chapter you have stood on carries the Pattern stamp of the day you found it, kept in your trail on this device; the rest wait for you on their own pages, in the order of the road.

The map is a story's map, drawn alongside the record — the record itself is unchanged by it.

The World Gate

The world, on the Atlas table

The Traveller's Map is now the world's map in fact: the thirteen lands of the Codex as a raised relief on the Atlas Room's long table, under today's sky. Today's sky is read live from the calendar when the Gate wakes. The Sphere turns above the table — it is the sky, not a land, and looking up is one tap. Every land below is a doorway: tap it on the relief, or take its name in plain words, and you travel to its page — every page keeps its own address, unchanged. The lands are drawn from the site's own catalog, so this map can never drift from the site.

    Aiyana’s World

    Not a land of the thirteen, but a door off the road, through the Clearing: a page Aiyana designed herself, where people can show their thinking, share how they feel, and tell their stories — under her own sign, with butterflies moving over it. Stories there wait for her yes before they can appear; that is her rule, and the page keeps it.
