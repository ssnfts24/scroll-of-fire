Spark Echo

Today's game is being read…

The Spark keeps the echo.

The Spark plays a short sequence. Play it back by tapping the stones. Every round adds one step, and the pace quickens gently. Later, two tones may sound together (tap both stones), and in free play a word may hide inside the lights. A miss costs nothing: hear it again, slower, or take a fresh sequence. There is no clock and nothing to lose.
