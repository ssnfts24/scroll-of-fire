Ethical Law

No field is coherent if it destroys the vessel carrying it.

Ethical Law is the containment layer of the Codex. It limits every equation, every practice, every claim, and every system. A method does not become acceptable merely because it is elegant, powerful, profitable, or internally consistent.

Ethics is not the last page of the theory. It is the boundary around every page.

Equation 9 · Containment

Ethical Law is a gate, not a decorative score.

\(\mathcal{E}_{9}(a,c)=\mathbf{1}[C \land D \land T \land P \land R \land S]\)

An action a in context c may continue only while consent, dignity, truthfulness, proportionality, responsibility, and a usable stop condition remain present. If any required condition fails, the gate closes; coherence, elegance, urgency, or claimed benefit does not override it.

Core Laws

Six conditions that travel with every system.

Consent

Participation is informed, specific, reversible, and free from covert pressure. “No” and “stop” are complete instructions.

Dignity

A person is never reduced to an input, audience, diagnosis, target, conversion, or proof object.

Truthfulness

Observation, interpretation, symbolic meaning, hypothesis, and established evidence remain clearly labeled.

Proportion

The intensity, duration, data collected, and claims made remain proportionate to the stated purpose and actual evidence.

Responsibility

Operators preserve provenance, disclose limits, correct errors, and remain answerable for foreseeable consequences.

Stop and Repair

Every practice has an immediate exit. Harm, coercion, distress, or unreliable conditions trigger pause, review, and repair.

Preflight and Review

Run the audit before, during, and after.

Before

  • State the purpose in plain language.
  • Confirm informed consent and privacy boundaries.
  • Identify risks, alternatives, and a clear stop action.
  • Separate symbolic exploration from evidence-bearing claims.

During

  • Watch for discomfort, coercion, confusion, or scope drift.
  • Keep volume, duration, data capture, and social pressure bounded.
  • Stop when the original conditions no longer hold.

After

  • Record what was observed and what remains uncertain.
  • Invite correction without punishment or status loss.
  • Repair harm, revoke unnecessary data, and revise the method.

The audit does not certify an action as harmless. It creates a reviewable minimum and keeps the burden of justification with the person proposing the action.
