Living Time · A guided practice

Read Your Own Day

Every day in the Codex has an address: a Moon, a day of the Moon, and a Pattern Day in the year. This short practice teaches you to read yours — not by reading about it, but by doing it, with the Spark leading and your own hands on the steps.

Four small steps, a few minutes: find today, see it on the Sphere, learn what a day holds, and witness this one. Nothing here is a test, and nothing is kept against you for days away.

The rules — all of them

What this practice keeps

  1. Only your place. The practice remembers which step you reached, in this browser, on this device — so the Spark can keep your place and welcome you back to it. That is all it stores.
  2. Your day's record is made on the day itself. This page writes no record for you. When you add a line in Day Notes, that note is a real record in your own Life Atlas — yours, on your device.
  3. One calendar, read live. Every number here comes from the same Pattern Calendar the Sphere and the 13 Moons page read, computed from your own local day at the moment you look. No second clock, no stored dates.
  4. Nothing is kept against you. No pace to hold, no count of missed days. Come back when you come back; the road keeps your place.
