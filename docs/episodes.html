The story, gathered in one room

The Episode Library

Every film of The Traveller’s Road that exists today stands in this one room, playable where it stands. The same films also live on their own pages, beside the written chapters they tell — travelling the site plays the story a piece at a time; this room is for sitting down with it.

Episodes are told apart the way the road itself tells them apart: by name, by land, by Moon, and by the token each one carries home — never by number. Under each film you will find its marks, its hum, the land it belongs to, and its kin on the road.

The films below are the site’s current cuts. A clean pass over the whole set — one Spark, one design, in every frame — is in production now; when its keeper has watched the finished set, the cleaned films will take these places. Nothing here is called final that is not.

Captions are not yet available for these films. Each episode’s written chapter is its reading companion: the chapter’s heart travels under its film here, and the chapter in full stands on the episode’s own page, linked from every card.

Your place is kept on this device only, by the same watch record the story’s own pages keep. An episode counts as watched when it has truly been played through — opening this page never counts. A finished watch brings the episode’s token home to your Spark, the same as watching it on its own page. Nothing is tallied, nothing can be lost, and nothing leaves your device.
