Get the app

Put the Codex on your home screen

The Codex of Reality can live on your phone like an app — its own icon, full screen, the Sphere and today one tap away. Installing takes under a minute, costs nothing, and asks for no account.

You are reading this inside the installed app right now — you're home.

Full screen, like an app

No browser bars. The Codex opens the way an app opens, on the front door, with the Sphere a tap away.

One tap to today

Your home-screen icon lands on today — the Moon, the Pattern Day, and the day waiting to be witnessed.

Your record stays yours

Reading and witnessing a day keeps its record on this device, exactly as it does in the browser. Installing changes the door, not the keeping.

Android — Chrome

  1. Tap Install the Codex above, if the button is lit.
  2. Or open Chrome's ⋮ menu and tap Install app (older Chrome says Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. The Codex appears with your other apps.

iPhone and iPad — Safari

  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up — at the bottom of Safari (on iPad, at the top).
  2. Scroll the sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The Codex appears on your home screen.

Apple gives web apps no install button of their own, so these three taps are the whole road — there is nothing to download and nothing to pay.

The fuller instrument: Spark. Living Day, the Android app, opens straight onto the Sphere, and its home-screen widget carries the day's plans beside a true picture of your Sphere. Its file lives in the Library. This home-screen install and the app sit happily side by side.

If the steps above don't appear, open codexofreality.org in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) — installing lives in the browser's own menu, not in any store, and never costs anything.
