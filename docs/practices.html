Remnant Lab · The Training Grounds

Practices

Experimental

The exercises wing of the Training Grounds: short drills for a human — breath, attention, seeing, memory, and intention — each one done fully here, in plain words, in a few quiet minutes. A school is not only lessons; it is practice. This room is the practice.

These are practices, not treatment, and nothing here is medicine or a promise of power. What each exercise trains is stated on its card, in ordinary words, and nothing beyond that is claimed. Where an exercise descends from an older tradition, the tradition's claim is labeled as its claim.

When you finish an exercise and seal it, the seal writes a practice record into your own Life Atlas — the same record a Manifest session at Teach writes. The day then counts as a practice day: it rains on your garden, and it carries its marker on the calendar. Leaving an exercise early writes nothing at all.

The exercises

Five doors, one for each family the wing opens with. More are designed and will be added family by family; the record they write is already the bench's own, so nothing about the room has to change for them to count.

🌬

Breath · About 3 minutes

The Fourfold Breath

Trains: breath pacing, and settling your attention on one counted rhythm.

The site's own ritual line, turned into a breath: Observe, Breathe, Align, Return — in for four, hold for four, out for four, rest for four, ten rounds, guided by a slow ring. Slow counted breathing is a plain, well-attested way to settle. Nothing more is claimed for it, and nothing more is needed.

🌀

Attention · About 3 minutes, plus your answers

Count the Returns

Trains: noticing that the mind wandered, and returning — the one move every attention practice is built on.

A small light drifts. Every twenty seconds you are asked one honest question: were you here? A return is a Drifted followed by a Here, and returns are what get counted. Drifting is not failure here — drifting is the weight, and returning is the rep. The count is your own honest tally, recorded as exactly that.

👁

Witness · One minute, plus one line

Sixty Seconds of Seeing

Trains: observation before interpretation — the Witness method, practised as a drill instead of read as a doctrine.

For sixty seconds, only noticing, in three layers: sounds, then the body, then light. Then one line — the one thing you noticed that you would have missed. That line is the fruit of the exercise, and it rides your record as its witness note.

🗝

Memory · About 2–3 minutes

The Keeping

Trains: deliberate recall, and choosing what to carry — memory practised as keeping, not storage.

Sit with today for a few breaths, then write three things from today you want to keep — one line each, no more. The day is reviewed while it is still warm, and the choosing is the exercise. Your lines stay in your own record, on this device.

🜚

Intention · About 3 minutes

The Sealed Intention

Trains: holding one pictured intention steady, and sealing it in one written line you can return to.

The small, plain sibling of the Manifest console at Teach. Four slow breaths; bring one specific thing to mind — a finished task, a room as it will be, a kindness you will do; hold it three ways — see it, hear it, feel its weight; then write the intention in one line and seal it.

The thought-power tradition claims thought shapes circumstance; that is the tradition's claim, and this bench keeps the stronger claim where it can be tested, at the Sending Test. What this exercise claims is smaller and true: a pictured, written intention is a commitment you made yourself, kept in your own record.

The rules — all of them

How practice counts here

  1. A record is written only at a seal. Finish an exercise, choose to seal it, and one practice record is kept in your Life Atlas, stamped with the day. Leave early, or let a finished exercise go unrecorded, and nothing is written — by design, not by loss.
  2. Practice is rain. A sealed practice day waters every garden bed you are growing, the same rain a Manifest session at Teach brings. The day also takes its practice marker on the 13 Moons calendar and in the Sphere's day view.
  3. Durations are real. The record carries the seconds between your own Begin and Seal taps — never the plan's estimate, never an invented number.
  4. Nothing is kept against you. No pace to hold, nothing counted down, nothing lost for days away. Come back when you come back; the room keeps your place.
  5. Your record, your device. Sealed practices are Life Atlas records. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; this room makes no network calls.

Neighbours

The wing stands in a row

And the strongest claim any of this touches — that intention acts at a distance — has its own instrument on the bench, where it is tested blind and scored against chance, honestly.
