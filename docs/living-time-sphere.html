Every year stays represented as a lightweight contour membrane around the unchanged 13-Moon anatomy. Past years move inward as record strata; future years move outward as planning and possibility strata, never as prediction. The selected Pattern day now cuts a gold corridor through every visible shell, with its Moon bounded in green, so the same coordinate can be compared across time. The chronology filament is structural navigation, not evidence. Gold equinox points and paths use only supported Alignment Ledger records, so unsupported years are never presented as measured astronomy.

                What happens to imported history?
                
Each record is normalized once into the Life Atlas with time, place, source, confidence, privacy, and Pattern coordinates. The Sphere then projects dated records onto the corresponding year membrane and Pattern-day angle. Duplicate fingerprints are skipped. Imported records are marked unreviewed so later reconciliation can confirm people, places, journeys, and merged events without silently rewriting source evidence.

Methods and coordinate rules

        

          
All coordinates are measured in degrees, clockwise from the top (12 o'clock = 0°). Zero position = Moon 1, Day 1 (Year Gate). Angles are deterministic: derived from structured data, not manually placed for visual appearance.
