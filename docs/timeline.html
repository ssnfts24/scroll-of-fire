Historical timeline

Two dated records on one page — and kept separate, because they are not the same kind of date.

How to read this page. The first lane is the record of the claims: the Codex's claim register cites published sources for some of its claims, and this lane is dated by when those sources were published. A source's publication date is not the date of the events a claim describes, and this page does not treat it as one.

The second lane is the growth of the Codex itself: every addition to this site is written into a growth record with its date, and that record is rendered here, oldest first.

Claim titles and evidence labels are the register's own words. This page changes no claim and no status. Claims whose register entries carry no dated source are not placed on the timeline — they are named at the bottom of this page, so the count stays honest.

Not yet dated

These claims stand in the register with no dated source attached, so no date can be given for them from the record, and they are not placed above. A claim joins the timeline only when a dated source is attached to it in the register.

    The Scroll's own timelines

    The Scroll itself contains timeline sections — a prophetic timeline from Genesis to the present, and timelines of suppression and fulfilment. Those are the author's own framings inside the work: statements of the Scroll, not independently verified events. They are kept apart from the dated records above, and can be read where they live, in the Scroll Library.
