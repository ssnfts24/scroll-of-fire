The Codex Guide

Ask a question in ordinary words. The Guide answers from this site's own record — its pages, its scrolls, its Hebrew Lexicon, and its Claim Register — and shows where every answer came from.

What the Guide is, plainly: a program that answers from the record. It is not Aaron, and it is not Witness. It cannot guess or speak for anyone, and it does not give legal, family, or personal counsel. Where the record has nothing, it says so in those words: the record doesn't cover that.

Asking the record never leaves your browser. The record it searches is the site's own, loaded as plain files, and the answering happens here on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no question is kept. Talking with the Guide — the section below, when it is switched on — is different: your question is sent to an AI company to be answered, and the section says so plainly before your first question.

Talk with the Guide

The Codex Guide — a program. Not Aaron, and not Witness. When talking is switched on, it answers from this site's record in conversation, under a short rulebook Aaron approved word for word, and shows the record every answer came from.

Before you ask: your question is sent to an AI company to be answered, and the answer comes back with the record passages it was drawn from. Your questions are not kept. Your own notes and records on this device never reach this page.

For children, the Guide stays the ask-the-record Guide above: scripted answers from the record, no open conversation, and nothing a child types leaves their device.

How the Guide stays true

Record only. Every answer is assembled from the same sealed index Living Search uses, generated from this site's own repository — so the Guide can never drift from the site it speaks for. Passages arrive with their pages; claims arrive as cards with what they establish, what remains unverified, and their evidence grade.

Calendar answers are computed, not searched. Ask "When is Pattern Day 216?" or "What day is Moon 4, Day 8?" and the Guide works it out with the Pattern Calendar — the same calendar the Sphere and the 13 Moons read.

Try it: "When is Pattern Day 216?" · "What does the record say about the Abraham Accords?" · "When is the Day Out of Time?"

A rulebook it cannot rewrite. The talking Guide runs under a short rulebook Aaron approved word for word: record only, the fence named in it, a monthly talking limit it cannot spend past, and no web searching — searching belongs to a keeper's own Spark, not to the public Guide. The rulebook changes only with Aaron's approval of the new wording, and the Guide checks its copy against the approved hash before it will talk.

Your own days are elsewhere. The Guide speaks for the site's public record only. Your notes, practice, and plans live on the 13 Moons page, in Day Notes, on your device. This page is built so those records cannot reach it — it loads none of their code.
