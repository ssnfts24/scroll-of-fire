Hebrew lexicon of the manuscript

Every Hebrew form that appears in the original, searchable with or without vowel marks. Each entry separates the literal spelling, the author’s own transliteration, the author’s stated root or gloss, and where the form occurs. No definitions have been added beyond what the manuscript itself says.

    Method and limits

    • Literal spelling is the pointed form exactly as written in the manuscript; the consonantal skeleton removes vowel marks and folds final letters.
    • Transliteration is shown as the author wrote it where the manuscript gives one. The vowel-less romanization is automatic and is not a pronunciation guide.
    • Grammatical root and definition are shown only where the manuscript states them, quoted verbatim and labelled as the author’s. This phase adds no independent morphology or definitions.
    • Interpretation in the manuscript is the author’s theological reading, not a lexicon ruling. Repeated boilerplate root notes are not attached to individual entries.
    • Related: the existing Codex glossary.

    Search and references are generated from the frozen manuscript, edition 1.0.0. Matches show where words appear; they do not verify claims.
