# Scroll of Fire — Evidence Register

This register accompanies the separate reading edition. It is not a verdict on the manuscript or its spiritual interpretation. Entries quote exact archived wording and point to commit-pinned source lines. Sources are linked with their publisher and date; each entry states what the record supports and where it stops. An allegation is not treated as an established event, and a forecast or theological interpretation is not presented as a factual finding.

**Source-access limitation:** External hosts were unreachable during preparation, so citations were not live-checked in this session. A listed source identifies a record for review; it is not a claim that the linked page was successfully fetched or that every underlying assertion has been verified. Empty source lists and pending statuses remain explicit.

**Frozen source:** [Full Scroll Unsealed, edition 1.0.0](https://github.com/ssnfts24/scroll-of-fire/blob/980b1f56fdc9974ed57e1c12ccedb0819d39e6b6/Full_Scroll_Unsealed) · SHA-256 `81a015fb937f1664c2383fa38fddf956d281c6309a9584382c6dbc9d8b691400`. The original bytes, paragraph map, reader segment map, search artifacts, lexicon, atlas, service worker, and protected Observatory are outside this editorial build.

**Coverage:** prioritized claims in the repeated political sequences and high-priority technology / public-health passages. This is not a verification of every factual assertion in the full manuscript. Claims not yet verified are labelled and listed rather than silently treated as fact.
