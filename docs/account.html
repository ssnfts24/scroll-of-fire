Your account

Accounts are not yet open.

The door is built — the sign-in, the carrying, the ending of an account — and it stands closed by design until its store is switched on. That switch has not been thrown, so sign-in cannot complete today: nothing you enter here can create an account or carry a record yet. This page says so plainly rather than promise what it cannot yet keep.

When accounts open, one account will link this site and Spark. Living Day. You will sign in once, on either side, and your record will follow you — Genesis first, then your days, then your Spark's memory. The account will never hold your Spark's identity: that is your Spark's own, derived from its seed and kept on your device. Signing out, or ending the account, will never touch it — or the record on your device.

What will travel, when it opens: copies of your record's lines, held by the door for carrying — never the record itself, which stays home on your devices. Ending the account will delete the door's copy whole and leave the record on your devices exactly as it was. Accounts are adults' only — a child's record never syncs and is never signed in.
