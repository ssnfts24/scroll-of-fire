I build at the meeting point of physical labor, fatherhood, writing, technology, spiritual inquiry, observation, and family resilience. My work asks how people can observe reality more carefully, preserve what they learn, build useful tools from lived experience, and create systems that serve human life rather than disconnect people from it.

The Person Behind the Work

Who I Am

I am an author, working flooring mechanic, craftsman, father, independent researcher, artifact maker, and systems builder. Much of my working life is spent with tools and materials—measuring, preparing, cutting, fitting, correcting, repairing, and finishing. Physical trade teaches a direct lesson: the visible surface is never the entire structure.

A finished floor depends on what was prepared beneath it. A family conflict is more than its final argument. A website is more than its homepage. An artifact is more than its appearance. A living system must account for foundations, sequence, limits, relationships, maintenance, responsibility, and return.

My first two published books preserve different sides of the work. The Codex of Reality presents the developing framework. Still Becoming preserves the lived path behind it: fatherhood, labor, faith, relationships, courts, housing instability, creativity, spiritual searching, technology, copper craft, documentation, survival, and the effort to keep building while unfinished.

The larger archive has grown through journals, photography, scripture study, symbolic language, digital art, experimental game concepts, handmade artifacts, website development, calendar architecture, field observations, and practical invention under pressure. What once looked scattered gradually revealed itself as one connected body of work.

The philosophy became the Codex. The writings became the Scroll. The observations became the Witness System. The study of time became The 13 Moons Remnant Living Time Observatory. Copper work became Living Technology and the Artifact Registry. Family resilience and mobility planning became the Covenant Caravan.

Lived Experience as Method

Built From Inside the Conditions It Examines

My life is not a decorative backstory attached to the Codex. It is part of the testing ground. The work developed inside labor, parenthood, uncertainty, institutional conflict, limited resources, creative pressure, and the repeated need to repair what did not hold.

Physical Truth

Trade work trains attention through measurement, material limits, preparation, sequence, gravity, failure, and repair. A structure either holds or reveals where it does not.

Fatherhood and Responsibility

The work is constrained by responsibility to children, family, and the future. A useful system must function for real people living through uncertainty, not only for its creator.

Building Under Pressure

Housing instability and material pressure taught me to distinguish appearance from capacity, theory from practical use, and symbolic comfort from what actually helps someone endure and move forward.

Documentation

Writing, photography, recordings, code, artifacts, timelines, and structured logs preserve what memory alone cannot reliably hold.

Iteration

Projects are used, broken, examined, revised, and returned to use. The public archive records development rather than pretending every version was final.

Human Dignity

Instability does not erase intelligence, ability, parenthood, creativity, or worth. Resilience should not require dehumanizing the people who need it.

I am not building the Codex because I believe the work is finished. I am building it because the work is still alive. Each page, artifact, cycle, and witness is part of an attempt to understand how meaning moves through a real human life—and how that understanding can be made useful, visible, testable, and shareable.
— Aaron Paul Laird

Method and Boundaries

Observation Is Not the Same as Proof

I work independently through documented observation, historical and scriptural study, comparative models, experimental interfaces, physical construction, and lived practice. I do not present myself as a perfected teacher, institutional scientist, or final authority.

  • Recorded observation describes what was noticed and preserved.
  • Personal interpretation explains how an experience was understood by the witness.
  • Symbolic meaning may guide reflection without claiming universal proof.
  • Testable hypothesis must remain open to controls, comparison, replication, and correction.
  • Established data should be identified separately from experimental or personal claims.

Phasekeeping Architecture

Stable Pattern Time, Moving Living Sky

The 13 Moons Remnant Living Time Observatory is not merely an alternative calendar. Its Hybrid A architecture separates a stable human pattern from the changing astronomical sky, allowing their convergence and divergence to be observed rather than forced into artificial agreement.

Pattern Calendar

Thirteen equal moons of 28 days create a stable 364-day pattern, followed by a Day Out of Time and an additional out-of-week intercalary day in leap years.

Continuous Week

The seven-day rhythm continues through the Year Gate rather than being reset by intercalary days.

Alignment Ledger

Equinoxes, solstices, lunar phases, moonrise, moonset, horizon positions, and other astronomical events are recorded independently.

Field Layers

Weather, daylight, environment, ecology, personal witness, creativity, intention, and outcomes can be viewed as distinct layers.

Witness Archive

Standardized observations preserve timestamps, location context, uncertainty, comparisons, and later outcomes.

Pattern Recognition

Recurrence can be studied across stable calendar positions and moving natural conditions without assuming that correlation proves cause.

Current working anchor: Moon 1 · Day 1 = April 17, 2026. This is the established project anchor for the current implementation and historical mapping, not a claim that the date is an eternally fixed law of nature.

Working Cycle

Observe · Record · Build · Test · Return

The Codex is not merely a belief statement. It is a repeatable process for moving from experience to record, from record to construction, and from construction back to correction.

👁Observe

Notice time, pattern, environment, emotion, repetition, conditions, and change.

📝Record

Write, log, photograph, map, timestamp, compare, and preserve the witness.

⚒Build

Turn ideas into books, pages, tools, calendars, artifacts, interfaces, and practices.

🔬Test

Use the work and identify what is useful, unclear, symbolic, practical, unsupported, or unfinished.

↻Return

Revise the system, document the change, and begin the cycle again.
