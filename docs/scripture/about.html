Limits

  • Versification: Hebrew (WLC) numbering is used; where English Bibles number differently (e.g. Gen 32, Exod 8) the verse shows the English reference.
  • No MT other than WLC, no Septuagint, Dead Sea Scrolls or Samaritan text is loaded. The reader does not assert variants from those witnesses; tradition notes citing them are marked “needs-source-check”.
  • Transliteration is mechanical and approximate (it does not distinguish every shewa or qamets). Ancient pronunciation is reconstructed and debated.
  • Consonantal display hides vowel and cantillation marks visually; the underlying text is unchanged. No Paleo-Hebrew view is offered.
  • The word-by-word tables list every word part with its OSHB lemma and morphology code.
  • Occurrence lists are references only; the Hebrew of other books is not loaded here.
