The standing word studies

A lemma is a word's dictionary form — the headword every occurrence of it gathers under. The detailed lemma records on this page are drawn live from the lexicon data when JavaScript runs. The Codex's fullest lemma studies stand here permanently, with or without JavaScript:

  • אָדָם — Adam: man, humankind. Genesis 1–5 worked sense by sense.
  • אֲדָמָה — Adamah: ground, land. The curse-and-lifting arc, Genesis 3 to 8.
  • אֱנוֹשׁ — Enosh: man as mortal.
  • רוּחַ — Ruach: wind, breath, spirit; the Genesis 1:2 dispute laid out.
  • נֶפֶשׁ — Nephesh: life, being, self.
  • אוֹר — Or: light; day-one light against day-four luminaries.
  • שֵׁם — Shem: name, reputation, the Name.
  • דָּבָר — Davar: word, thing, matter; the Ten Words and the word that does not return empty.

For any other word: the Lexicon searches 911 Hebrew forms, and every chapter page carries a static word-by-word table with the lemma id and morphology of every verse — open a chapter from the Scripture home page and scroll to its table.
