Context: The burning bush and the divine name (draft, unreviewed)

Literary observations

  • The name-revelation (3:13-15) turns on the verb hayah ('to be'); 'ehyeh' is first person singular imperfect.

Background

  • 'ehyeh asher ehyeh' (3:14) is translated in several ways ('I am who I am', 'I will be what I will be', 'I will be who I will be'), because the imperfect can express present or future.

Where interpreters differ

  • How should 'ehyeh asher ehyeh' be translated?
    • I AM WHO I AM (common English rendering)
    • I WILL BE WHAT I WILL BE (common alternative; reflects the imperfect)
