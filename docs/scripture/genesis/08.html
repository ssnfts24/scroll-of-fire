God remembered Noah, all the animals, and all the livestock that were with him in the ship; and God made a wind to pass over the earth. The waters subsided.

Context notes for this verse (draft, unreviewed)
  • H7307 (draft-unreviewed): Here ruach is the object of a causative verb of passing over the earth, in a sentence about the floodwaters subsiding; a natural wind is the reading the immediate clause supports. This does not exclude a theological reading of the cause.
