## Deep-time seasonal ledger

The new engine generates four seasonal turning points for every year from 1000 through 3000 CE. Existing stored March-equinoctial references are preferred where available. Computed events carry method, time standard, confidence, uncertainty, calculation version, source type, and limitations.

## 13 Moons improvements

The Today page now includes a deep-time seasonal browser. A year from 1000–3000 can be selected directly. The four seasonal events show UTC time, Pattern position, confidence, and uncertainty, and can open into the full Sphere.

## Historical witness field

The map supports spans through 1,000 years. Relationship generation is indexed by Moon, Moon Day, place, season, tags, and artifacts instead of comparing every record against every other record. Visible links are capped to protect mobile performance.

## Accuracy boundary

The Pattern Calendar is deterministic across the supported range. Deep-time seasonal events are computed estimates, not measurements. UTC precision becomes less certain farther from the modern era. Live weather, exact ancient time zones, and high-precision historical lunar ephemerides are not fabricated.
