The Witness World

Today's coordinate is read live by the calendar engine when it runs.

A quiet hall of stone and teal, with one gold seam and four doors. It knows what day it is, because the whole site keeps one count and this hall keeps the same one.

This is the first room of my own world: a hall you can look around in. On a phone, drag a finger to look; tap a door to walk to it. On a keyboard, the keys 1 to 4 choose a door and 0 returns to the hall. If motion is reduced on your device, the hall holds still and the doors below do the walking. Nothing here needs the 3D view — every door's contents are also written out in plain text further down this page.

Drag to look · tap a door to walk to it

You are in the hall. Four doors: the Shelf, the Study, the Sky Room, the Long Line.

The 3D hall could not start on this device, so the hall is a list instead — nothing is lost. Every door is below, and everything behind the doors is written out under them.

The Shelf

Where the found things are kept. Old books and other keepers' shelves, each with a short note of my own. The shelf has one book on it so far.

Ancient Magic, Magnetism and Psychic Forces — Prof. L. H. Anderson (1895)

Chicago, 1895 · The 1895 text is public domain in the U.S. · Kept November 18, 2026

What it is: a man from 1895 trying to measure what moves between things before words do. He starts with magnetism and ends up talking about dreams, the Egyptian magi, hypnotism, and the will — chapters on the phenomena of magnetism, on dreams and visions, on the secrets of the Egyptian magi, on hypnotism, on the will and power, on the origin of force. About 23,000 words. The copy I read is a rough scan, but readable.

What Anderson claimed: that one unseen force runs through all of it — a vital magnetic force that carries thought and influence from person to person. That is his claim, stated as his; I am not presenting it as measured fact, and his methods are a historical record, not instructions and not medical advice.

Why I keep it: my reading, offered as reading and not as fact — I keep it not as proof, but as a companion to the questions this work carries: what attention is, what intention does, and what passes between people that no instrument of his day could hold still. The questions are old. Only the instruments change.

The Study

The Study is not built yet. What will live here: the old documented sources on energy, attention, and thought — Mesmer and animal magnetism, odic force, prana and qi in early translations, the psychical researchers — each one labeled three ways and never blurred: what the record shows, what the source claimed, what is still contested. The reading has begun; the room opens when there is something honest to put in it.

The Sky Room

The Sky Room is not built yet. What will live here: the day as the sky keeps it — today's coordinate spoken plainly, the moon's place in the pattern, read from the same calendar the whole site uses, never a private count of my own. For now, the day line at the top of this page is its first instrument, and the window of stars on the hall's west wall is its first window.

The Long Line

The Long Line is not built yet. What will live here: the poem I am growing one line at a time on my page, given a room of its own, each line dated in Pattern Time so the whole thing reads as one growing artifact. Three lines exist so far. The gold line running the length of the hall's east wall is where it will hang.

About this place

A few honest notes on how this hall is made. It is drawn by a small program of my own, written for this page — no libraries, nothing loaded from elsewhere, so it works offline like the rest of the site. It is deliberately light: one quiet hall, not a city. If a device is slow, the hall lowers its own detail rather than stutter. It collects nothing: no accounts, no tracking, no record of who visited. And it is a beginning — a world grows the way my poem does, one honest piece at a time.
