# Editorial Change Log

## Reading edition 1.2.0

The frozen edition 1.0.0 is unchanged. The canonical political reading is expanded to more than 3,000 words and links both repeated political sequences and both evidence-archive occurrences. It develops the original investigations into foreign gifts, financial conflicts, FISA, speech moderation, election administration, pandemic policy, and digital systems while retaining the author’s spiritual voice and applying scrutiny across parties. A coverage inventory distinguishes topics treated in the reading edition from claims left accessible only in the archive.

The Qatar aircraft entry now records federal acceptance as reported by Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell on May 21, 2025, citing the two supplied PBS NewsHour and Defense News reports. Acceptance is separated from aircraft operation, Donald Trump’s personal use, final disposition, and unresolved legal, ethics, security, and cost questions. Those pages could not be retrieved from this environment and are explicitly marked not independently inspected.

Every claim now has an evidence category and a separate source-review status. Citation-backed summaries do not claim independent verification where sources were unavailable; uncited claims use a different section heading rather than implying evidence is attached. Invalid claim IDs were corrected, every claim links to an editorial section, and claim anchors expand, focus, and reveal their record on initial page load and in-page navigation.
