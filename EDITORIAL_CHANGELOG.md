# Editorial Change Log

## Reading edition 1.1.0

The frozen edition 1.0.0 is unchanged. The new political reading edition consolidates the substantially repeated political sequence into one canonical presentation and links all original political and evidence-archive occurrences. No archival occurrence is removed; the original reader and search index continue to expose the complete source.

Substantive changes are recorded by claim ID in [EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md](EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md) and in [the machine-readable register](docs/scroll/evidence/claim-register.json). In summary, the edit removes political-leader-as-savior language; applies the same evidentiary standard across administrations, parties, agencies, media, companies, and donors; corrects attribution and scope where primary records establish it; and labels unverified claims, forecasts, and theological interpretation as such.

Original comparisons are included with each evidence record as exact quotations and commit-pinned source links. This log describes the reading-edition changes, not revisions to the manuscript or historic integrity ledgers.
